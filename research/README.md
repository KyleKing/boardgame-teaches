# Research

The evidence base for designing, evaluating, and improving Teaches. Chapters are numbered by dependency: principles first, our format experiments second, then the survey chapters, then the synthesis that the teach sheets actually build on.

1. [Pedagogy principles](01-principles.md): cognitive load, progressive disclosure, the coached first turn, the strategy-and-agency split, and why good teaches are rare
1. [Script styles](02-script-styles.md): verbatim vs beats vs cues tested on one game, the three content types, self-evident rules over edge-case tables, and the structural conventions
1. [History and formats](03-history-and-formats.md): case-system wargame rules to FFG's Learn to Play split, tutorial-first products, digital layers, and "the teach" becoming hobby jargon
1. [Video teaches](04-video-teaches.md): Watch It Played's rulebook-to-script rewrite, the other formats' tricks, video vs print vs live, and what survives translation
1. [Interactive teaching](05-interactive-rolling-ttrpg.md): the John Company rolling teach, Jaws of the Lion's tutorial campaign and its late-joiner gap, the TTRPG pattern, and other attested methods
1. [Synthesis and evaluation](06-evaluation.md): the best teaches by category, teach vs scripted round resolved, a 12-point rubric, the post-teach improvement loop, and open design space

Sourcing notes: BGG pages block automated fetching, so BGG-sourced details rest on search snippets and one browser-verified thread; they're marked in context where second-hand.
